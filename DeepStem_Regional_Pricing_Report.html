📖 The Philosophy Behind Regional Pricing

DeepStem Pro is built on the belief that professional digital tools should be accessible to everyone, regardless of where they live or their economic circumstances. Regional pricing is not about devaluing the product, it's about removing barriers so that DJs, producers, and musicians in lower-income countries can access the same quality tools as those in wealthier nations.

💡 The Core Principle: A DJ in Nigeria shouldn't have to pay the same as a DJ in London when the average income is ~30x lower. Fair pricing creates opportunity, drives progression, and builds sustainable global communities.

This report lists all 175 App Store countries, grouped by discount tier, with economic justifications based on GDP per capita (2025) and World Bank income classifications. The discounts are designed to be progressive : the lower the average income, the higher the discount.

💡 A Message to Every Creator

🚀 Opportunity

By making DeepStem Pro affordable in lower-income countries, you are unlocking creative potential for millions of aspiring DJs, producers, and musicians who otherwise couldn't afford professional tools. A DJ in Nigeria, a producer in Indonesia, or a musician in India can now access the same quality tools as someone in London or New York.

📈 Progression

Access to professional tools enables skill development and career progression. When creators in emerging markets can use the same tools as their counterparts in developed countries, they can compete on a level playing field. This drives global musical diversity and elevates local music scenes onto the world stage.

♻️ Sustainability

Fair pricing creates sustainable communities. When users feel valued and fairly treated, they become loyal customers, advocates, and contributors to the product's ecosystem. This builds a global, sustainable user base that grows organically, reducing churn and increasing lifetime value.
